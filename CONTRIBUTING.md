# Contributing

This is primarily an individual learning and research repository. Contributions should improve academic quality, reproducibility, or usability.

## Standards
- Cite primary sources and verify links.
- Do not invent institutional course details or bibliographic metadata.
- Distinguish proposed curriculum from completed learning.
- Qualify technical claims appropriately.
- Do not add proprietary, confidential, or restricted data.
- Use readable Markdown and descriptive filenames.
- Include reproducibility instructions for computational work.

## Review checklist
Is the change accurate and sourced? Does it fit program scope and workload? Are prerequisites and outcomes clear? Can another reader reproduce the analysis? Are assumptions, limitations, and licensing addressed?
