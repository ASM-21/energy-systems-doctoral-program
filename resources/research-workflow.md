# Research Workflow

## 1. Question selection
Score candidate questions on importance, novelty relative to literature, data availability, identification/modeling feasibility, time, and potential contribution. Do not begin with a preferred conclusion.

## 2. Literature review
For each paper record citation/DOI, question, theory, data/sample, method/identification, findings, limitations, relevance, and follow-up references. Search Google Scholar, EconLit, IEEE Xplore, NBER, and relevant government/RTO publications. Follow backward and forward citations. Distinguish peer-reviewed work from working papers and reports.

## 3. Research design
Before substantial analysis, write a design note stating question/hypothesis, estimand or engineering quantity, unit of observation, sample/time period, data sources, model equations, identifying assumptions, confounders, robustness/falsification tests, and limitations.

## 4. Data and analysis
Preserve raw data, document transformations, version code, validate units/time alignment, start with descriptive analysis, use the simplest adequate model, check assumptions, and perform sensitivity analysis. Separate exploratory from confirmatory claims.

## 5. Writing
Recommended paper structure: abstract; introduction/contribution; institutional/technical background; literature; conceptual framework; data/methods; results; robustness; discussion/policy implications; limitations/conclusion; references; reproducibility appendix.

## 6. AI use
AI may assist with search terms, code scaffolding, explanations, editing, and critique. It is not a source of record. Verify citations, calculations, code, and claims against original sources. Maintain an AI-use log for substantial projects. Do not submit confidential information without authorization.

## 7. Publication readiness
Conduct a systematic literature check; confirm contribution and venue; validate data rights and reproducibility; test robustness; obtain external technical feedback where possible; follow venue-specific ethics and formatting requirements.
