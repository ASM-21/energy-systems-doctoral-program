# Department: Regulation, Government, and Public Policy

## Scope
Legal, institutional, and economic frameworks governing energy infrastructure, wholesale markets, utilities, siting, reliability, and public investment.

## Competencies
Federal/state jurisdiction and roles of FERC, DOE, EPA, NERC, RTOs/ISOs, utilities, and state commissions; natural monopoly, cost-of-service regulation, rate base, allowed return, prudence, revenue requirements, and rate design; cost allocation and affordability; transmission planning, siting, interconnection, and jurisdictional boundaries; administrative process and regulatory evidence; policy design, implementation, and evaluation.

## Primary-source method
For every regulatory claim, identify the statute, regulation, order, tariff, or commission decision. Record docket number, issuing body, date, relevant section, and subsequent status. Secondary summaries are orientation, not final authority.

## Research questions
Do utility incentives align with least-cost, reliable infrastructure investment? How are interconnection costs allocated, and what incentives follow? How do state policies interact with regional wholesale-market rules? How can transparency and public participation improve without compromising technical integrity?
