# Department: Climate, Sustainability, and Energy Transition

## Scope
Climate externalities, decarbonization pathways, lifecycle impacts, technology deployment, environmental policy, and system-level trade-offs of energy transitions.

## Competencies
Greenhouse-gas accounting, emissions factors, and lifecycle assessment; marginal versus average emissions; carbon pricing, standards, subsidies, clean-energy mandates, and industrial policy; renewable integration, storage, transmission, demand response, and firm low-carbon resources; electrification, industrial decarbonization, embodied supply-chain emissions; reliability, affordability, land use, environmental justice, and distributional analysis; scenario analysis and uncertainty.

## Methodological cautions
Do not conflate nameplate capacity with energy production, annual average emissions with marginal emissions, or lifecycle impacts with operational emissions. State boundaries, counterfactuals, temporal resolution, and system effects. Treat scenarios as conditional projections, not forecasts unless a forecasting design is justified.

## Research questions
How do regional marginal emissions change the value of flexible load and storage? What are the reliability and cost consequences of alternative decarbonization pathways? How do domestic supply-chain policies alter lifecycle emissions and competitiveness?
