Use this checklist when creating or revising a course. Review the course page and its linked assignments, solutions, labs, and source material together. A polished overview page cannot compensate for missing or incorrect learning materials.

## Review disposition

Choose one:

- **Ready for active study:** no unresolved critical correctness, safety, integrity, or reproducibility issue.
- **Usable with limitations:** the course can be studied, but identified gaps must be visible to the learner.
- **Planned, not ready:** architecture exists, but essential instructional or validation materials are incomplete.
- **Requires correction:** a material error or misleading claim blocks use.

### Critical blockers

A course must not be marked ready for active study if it contains a known incorrect worked solution, broken essential link, untraceable empirical claim, unsafe data practice, or a computational result that cannot be reproduced and has no disclosed limitation.
