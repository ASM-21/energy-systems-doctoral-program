# Year 1 — Integrated Foundations

**Workload:** 3–5 hours/week. Four modular courses, taken sequentially or with limited overlap. Each is designed for 8–12 weeks at the stated pace; extend as needed rather than rushing.

| Course | Focus | Main output |
|---|---|---|
| Y1-01 | Power Systems and Electricity Infrastructure | Power-system model and technical report |
| Y1-02 | Energy Economics and Market Fundamentals | Dispatch/welfare analysis |
| Y1-03 | Electricity Markets, Utility Regulation, and PJM | Regulatory/market structure memo |
| Y1-04 | Applied Quantitative Methods | Reproducible empirical analysis |

Complete at least 80% of core work, all major assessments, and the final project. Demonstrate outcomes using the rubric in ACADEMIC_STANDARDS.md. Record actual hours and evidence in progress/.

## Integration project
At year end, select one focused question on infrastructure, DERs, interconnection, utility incentives, or PJM. Produce a 2,000–3,000 word literature/institutional review, documented dataset or transparent model, empirical/engineering analysis, 1,000–1,500 word policy brief, reproducibility package, and presentation.
