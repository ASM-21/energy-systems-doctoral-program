# Y1-03 — Electricity Markets, Utility Regulation, and PJM

**Level:** Graduate / applied institutional analysis | **Duration:** 10–12 weeks | **Effort:** 35–45 hours  
**Prerequisites:** Y1-02 or equivalent microeconomics and basic electricity-system literacy.

## Purpose
Understand institutions and rules governing U.S. wholesale electricity markets and regulated utilities. PJM and Pennsylvania are recurring cases, not proxies for every region.

## Learning outcomes
Explain roles of FERC, state commissions, RTOs/ISOs, NERC, utilities, and market monitors; distinguish energy, capacity/resource adequacy, ancillary services, and transmission rights; explain day-ahead/real-time markets and LMP; interpret utility revenue requirements and retail rates; identify federal/state jurisdiction; analyze how interconnection, transmission planning, and cost allocation shape investment; write source-supported regulatory analysis.

## Primary sources
- FERC, *Wholesale Electricity Markets: Overview and Guide*: [official guide](https://www.ferc.gov/wholesale-electricity-markets-overview-and-guide).
- PJM manuals and tariffs: [manuals](https://www.pjm.com/library/manuals); PJM data: [Data Miner](https://dataminer2.pjm.com/).
- PJM market-monitor reports: [Monitoring Analytics](https://www.monitoringanalytics.com/reports/PJM_State_of_the_Market/).
- FERC dockets: [eLibrary](https://elibrary.ferc.gov/).
- Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission: [PUC](https://www.puc.pa.gov/).
- NERC reliability publications: [NERC](https://www.nerc.com/pa/RAPA/ra/Pages/default.aspx).

## Assessments
Institutional map with jurisdiction boundaries; simplified energy-market clearing and LMP exercise; analysis of a PJM manual/tariff section with exact version/effective date; simplified revenue-requirement and rate-design model; final analysis of a market/infrastructure issue using primary sources.

## Final project options
PJM interconnection and cost allocation; transmission expansion; capacity/resource adequacy and load growth; DER aggregation; data-center connection and cost allocation; Pennsylvania utility investment incentives and rate design.

For every tariff/regulatory claim, record exact document title, docket/manual section, version/effective date, URL, and access date. Press releases and news are not substitutes for controlling documents.

## Completion evidence
Institutional map, worked market exercises, regulatory source log, 2,000–3,000 word report, and one-page policy brief.
