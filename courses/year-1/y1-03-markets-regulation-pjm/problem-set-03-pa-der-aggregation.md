# Y1-03 Problem Set 3 — Pennsylvania DER Aggregation and Regulatory Boundaries

**Estimated time:** 3–4 hours  
**Casebook:** [Pennsylvania DER interconnection and PJM wholesale aggregation](../../../research/casebooks/pa-der-interconnection/README.md)  
**Primary sources:** PPL Rule 12 (PUC No. 202, Supplement No. 2); PA PUC NOPR in L-2023-3044115; FERC Order 2222; PJM DER Aggregator Participation Model materials.

The Chapter 57 provisions discussed here are proposed rules, not final law. Your analysis must maintain that distinction throughout.

## Problem 1 — Classify the decisions

For each action below, identify the principal decision-maker and governing instrument. Explain where responsibilities overlap.

1. A customer applies to physically connect a PV system to a PPL distribution feeder.
2. A DERA seeks to include the approved PV system in a PJM wholesale aggregation.
3. PJM evaluates registration and wholesale market eligibility of the DERA.
4. The distribution utility determines whether a component DER's operation could adversely affect safe and reliable distribution-system operation.
5. The utility needs to override a wholesale dispatch signal because it would jeopardize distribution reliability.

Distinguish physical interconnection approval from approval to participate as a Component DER in a wholesale aggregation.

## Problem 2 — Net-metering and wholesale compensation

Under the April 11, 2026 proposed Chapter 57 NOPR, describe the proposed treatment of a customer-generator receiving service under an EDC net-metering tariff for:
- wholesale energy markets;
- wholesale capacity markets;
- wholesale ancillary-services markets.

Explain the proposed conditions and the purpose of the anti-double-compensation rule. Identify the proposed "release valve" and why it requires both EDC confirmation and PJM approval.

## Problem 3 — Data access and privacy

A DERA requests historical usage and operating data for a residential component DER. Explain:
1. What data-access mechanism the NOPR proposes.
2. Why customer/operator consent matters.
3. Why this proposal does not create a public feeder-level dataset.
4. What confidentiality, cybersecurity, standardization, and audit questions a final rule or tariff should address.

## Problem 4 — Review timeline and cost recovery

The proposed NOPR contemplates an electronic EDC review process with a 60-day review period and allows an EDC to propose fees through a tariff filing.

Explain why:
- the 60-day period should not be represented as a general DER interconnection timeline;
- a DERA participation review is different from an interconnection impact study;
- fees should be assessed for cost causation, administrative burden, and barriers to entry;
- review reasons and data requirements should be standardized enough to support predictability without eliminating necessary utility-specific engineering judgment.

## Problem 5 — Policy memo

Write a 1,000–1,500 word memo evaluating whether Pennsylvania's proposed framework appropriately balances:
- distribution safety and reliability;
- wholesale competition and DER market access;
- retail customer consent and privacy;
- prevention of duplicative compensation;
- cost allocation and ratepayer protection;
- transparency and administrative burden.

Your memo must identify at least three provisions that are proposed, not final. It must separately discuss PPL Rule 12 and the PPL Second DER Management Plan.

## Submission

- Decision-rights matrix.
- Net-metering participation crosswalk.
- Data-access flow diagram.
- Policy memo with primary-source citations and effective/status dates.
