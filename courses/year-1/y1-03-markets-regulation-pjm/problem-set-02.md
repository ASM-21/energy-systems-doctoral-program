# Y1-03 Problem Set 2 — Utility Revenue Requirement and Rate Design

## Problem 1 — Simplified revenue requirement

A regulated utility has:
- rate base: $500 million;
- debt financing: $200 million at 5%;
- equity financing: $300 million at an allowed 9% return;
- annual depreciation: $10 million;
- O&M expense: $25 million;
- taxes and other allowed costs: $5 million;
- other revenue credits: $2 million.

1. Calculate the weighted cost of capital.
2. Calculate the allowed return on rate base.
3. Calculate the simplified annual revenue requirement.
4. Explain why the revenue requirement is not identical to a customer's volumetric energy price.
5. Identify regulatory judgments and risks that are absent from this arithmetic.

## Problem 2 — Rate design

A utility must recover $100 million annually from 100,000 customers. Compare:
- a uniform fixed charge;
- a volumetric charge based on kWh;
- a demand charge based on coincident or noncoincident kW;
- a time-of-use rate.

For each, discuss cost causation, bill volatility, conservation incentives, equity, revenue stability, and implementation/data requirements. Explain why a tariff can be cost-recovering but still produce inefficient incentives.

## Problem 3 — Cost allocation and beneficiaries

A $120 million network upgrade is triggered by a new large load but also improves reliability and enables future development.

Develop at least three allocation approaches. Identify who benefits, who causes costs, who bears stranded-asset risk, and how uncertainty should be handled. Explain why engineering causation and regulatory cost allocation are related but not identical.

## Problem 4 — Regulatory source memo

Choose a current Pennsylvania PUC order, FERC order, tariff provision, or PJM manual section relevant to one of the topics above. Cite exact sections and effective dates. Explain the legal authority, affected parties, implementation mechanism, and one unresolved policy trade-off.

## Submission

Provide a calculation workbook/script, tariff comparison matrix, 1,000-word memo, and a source appendix. This exercise is conceptual and does not substitute for a jurisdiction-specific rate case analysis.
