## Research standards
- Use public, traceable data only.
- Preserve source provenance, effective dates, units, and transformation logic.
- Do not attribute state/sector/balancing-authority aggregates to an individual utility.
- Separate utility claims, party testimony, proposed findings, approved settlement terms, Commission findings, and independently measured outcomes.
- Treat every data-availability claim as a hypothesis until the raw files and schema are inspected.
