# Research Casebooks

Casebooks are structured exercises in reading primary documents and connecting rules to technical and economic decisions.

- [Pennsylvania DER interconnection and PJM wholesale aggregation](pa-der-interconnection/README.md) — a versioned case study of distribution interconnection, DERMS, Order 2222, and PJM's DER Aggregator Participation Model.

Casebooks should identify the exact version, effective date, legal status, and jurisdiction of every source. Orientation documents and slide decks are not substitutes for controlling tariffs, orders, or manuals.
