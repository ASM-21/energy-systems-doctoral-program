# Research Question Bank

Questions are candidates, not approved projects. Each requires a literature review, data-feasibility test, and methodological critique before adoption.

## Distribution systems and DERs
- Which public indicators can characterize distribution interconnection friction, and where are the largest data gaps?
- How do distribution upgrade-cost allocation rules change incentives for DER developers and ratepayers?
- How should hosting-capacity information be interpreted by developers, utilities, and regulators?
- What is the value of non-wires alternatives under uncertain load growth and equipment lead times?

## Utility regulation and investment
- Under what conditions do cost-of-service incentives encourage or discourage timely grid investment?
- How do performance-based mechanisms alter utility incentives, and what are the measurement problems?
- How do regulators evaluate prudence when forecasts, equipment costs, and project schedules change?
- What are the trade-offs between rate stability, cost reflectivity, and efficient electrification?

## Wholesale markets and reliability
- How do energy, capacity, and ancillary-service revenues jointly affect resource investment?
- How do transmission constraints change the distribution of benefits from generation and storage?
- What are the reliability and market consequences of concentrated load growth?
- How do market rules perform under correlated weather, fuel, and outage risks?

## Decarbonization and industrial policy
- How do hourly and locational emissions signals alter the value of flexible loads?
- What are the system costs of alternative pathways for firm low-carbon capacity?
- How do supply-chain localization policies trade off resilience, cost, and lifecycle emissions?
- How does electricity availability affect industrial siting and regional competitiveness?

## Data centers and large loads
- How should large-load interconnection costs and stranded-asset risks be allocated?
- What operational flexibility can data centers provide, and what contractual barriers limit it?
- How do load forecasts respond to uncertainty in AI compute demand, efficiency, and siting?
- What reliability planning approaches are robust to clustered, high-density load additions?

## Candidate scoring

Score each dimension 1–5 and explain:
- significance;
- novelty relative to literature;
- data availability/quality;
- identification or engineering-model feasibility;
- tractability at 3–5 hours/week;
- usefulness to a real decision-maker.

A candidate with weak data or identification should be reframed as an institutional, engineering, or methodological study rather than forced into an empirical causal paper.
